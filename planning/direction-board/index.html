1 · What changed in the system

  • Ink: black #111111 for all text, controls and drawings on white. Dark olive leaves the website system (kept for print only if you want it).
  • Colour fields: whole sections take a palette colour: church field in apricot, barn field in sage, photos on ivory, closing on sage. Drawings on a field are white; text on a field stays black (contrast 7.6:1 on sage, 8.2:1 on apricot, 9.6:1 on peony, 12:1 on butter).
  • Sticker treatment: on ivory panels, drawings may take a palette colour with a thin white die-cut outline (programme landmarks). On white, drawings are black.
  • The road is now a black dashed hand-drawn line. It starts under the hero illustration, runs through the programme and ends at the closing.
  • Hero: centred and minimal: one drawing, names, date, places, one sentence, one action. The start of the story.
  • Panel grammar (from your “Love Doodles” references): every section is a flat panel with one ground and one ink. White + black, ivory + a palette colour, or a palette colour + ivory. Headings are bold and set in the panel’s ink; one big drawing per panel; triptychs where two places or three facts sit side by side.
  • Unchanged: one illustration family (whimsical), Fraunces + DM Sans, white as the dominant ground (now about 70 % of surfaces, the rest palette fields), one signature motion.
