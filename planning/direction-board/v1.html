1 · Concept

Chvíle, ktoré sa oplatí zdieľať.

The couple's own line, „Ľúbime chvíle, ktoré môžeme zdieľať spolu,“ becomes the whole page: the wedding day is a sequence of moments, and every guest is being asked to share them. The programme is therefore not a schedule but a walk through the day: from the church in Hlinné along one drawn footpath into the garden at Zlatník, with each moment a landmark on the path.

Working title: „Chvíle“ (Moments). Starting hypothesis „Májová cesta“ survives as the path grammar, but the subject is the moments, not the geography.

  • Ground: white, 85–90 % of surfaces. One warm ivory field per screen at most (hero art, photo moment).
  • Ink: dark olive for all text, controls and drawings. Drawings are recoloured to the ink, never left black.
  • Accent: sage carries the route and small identity marks. One floral accent (peony pink) per composition; apricot only where it means the church.
  • Illustration: whimsical family only, 14 selected drawings, shown large (150–560 px). No stickers, no icon rows.
  • Type: Fraunces (names, headings, times at display size) + DM Sans (body, controls, captions).
  • Path grammar: one continuous hand-drawn footpath: a soft sage bed with a dashed sage centre line. It connects landmarks, never overlaps copy, and is redrawn from the real milestone positions.
  • Photo treatment: full colour, square corners, same aspect ratio within a group, no filters, one gentle depth drift at most.
  • Motion grammar: one signature move (the route reveals on scroll), section entrances in reading order, control feedback 140–180 ms. Reduced motion shows the complete static route.
  • Print extension: same ink, same drawings, same type on ivory stock: invitation, programme card (the path in one column) and place cards (one drawing + name).

The list is a semantic <ol> fed by one typed events array (id, title, description, time or pending status, place, illustration). The route is a separate decorative SVG whose geometry is computed from the real milestone positions after layout and on resize, so copy, fonts and viewport can change without clipped loops or overlaps. Desktop alternates text left/right around a central landmark column, reading strictly top to bottom. On phones the route runs down the left beside a single column. The signature motion is the route drawing itself in as you scroll (CSS scroll-driven animation, transform-free); where unsupported or with reduced motion the complete route is simply there.

Alt 1 · „Dve miesta, jeden deň“The page is built on the two buildings: the church in its real apricot and the barn in sage bracket every screen, and everything in the programme is colour-coded to the place where it happens. Clearest information design, more graphic and less tender than „Chvíle“. Shares the same type, ink and drawings, so switching is cheap.

Alt 2 · „Pozvánka na stole“The website behaves like the printed invitation suite laid on an ivory table: a white card for the invitation, a long programme card with the path, an RSVP card, an envelope for contact. Uses the pack's stationery drawings and makes the print extension literal. The most “invitation-like”, but ivory becomes the dominant ground, which conflicts with the white-first brief unless the cards grow to fill the screen.
